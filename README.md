# Highway Drive

A free, offline 3D endless highway driving game for Android, made by Tasin ([@Tasin202](https://github.com/Tasin202)). Get in your car, pick a road and traffic, and just drive.

## Download and install
1. Go to the **Releases** page of this repository and download `HighwayDrive.apk`.
2. Open the file on your phone. If Android asks, allow installs from this source ("Install unknown apps").
3. Tap **Install**, then open **Highway Drive**.

**Requirements:** Android 8.0 or newer. Works on 32-bit and 64-bit ARM phones. No internet needed.

## Features
- Endless procedurally generated roads: straights, curves, hills and tunnels
- One-way and two-way roads, with real oncoming traffic
- Four difficulties, from relaxed Easy to realistic, imperfect Extreme traffic
- Traffic drivers with different personalities: calm, cautious, aggressive, distracted
- Eight cars with different speed, handling, braking and weight
- Car shop, garage, customization and performance upgrades
- Six maps: City Highway, Countryside, Mountain, Desert, Coastal and Night City
- Morning, afternoon, evening and night, plus clear, cloudy and rain
- Endless, Time Attack and Free Drive modes
- Earn money from distance, sustained speed, overtakes and near misses
- First-person cockpit view with analog gauges, or third-person view
- Three steering options: on-screen wheel, touch, or tilt
- Graphics options for weaker and stronger phones
- Everything saves on your device

## Controls
- **Steering:** drag the on-screen wheel, hold the left or right side of the screen, or tilt your phone (choose in Settings).
- **GAS / BRAKE:** the pedal buttons. Hold brake when stopped to reverse.
- **HORN:** button at the top of the screen.
- **Pause:** top right. You can change the camera and steering there.

## Tips
- Use the Low or Medium graphics preset on older phones.
- Keep the speed up: sustained fast driving earns bonus money.
- A hard crash ends your run, so brake early in dense traffic.

## Known issues
- Cars are generated in the game, so they look stylized and not like real car models.
- Tilt steering may need calibration or the Invert option, depending on your phone.
